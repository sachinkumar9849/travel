Our Story

Connecting People with the Great Outdoors

Founded in 1998, Wanderlust Travel began with a simple idea: to provide transformative wilderness experiences that inspire a deeper connection with nature. What started as a small operation with two rafts has blossomed into one of the most respected adventure travel companies in the world.

We believe that the best way to protect our wild places is to share them responsibly. Every trip we design is crafted to challenge, inspire, and foster a sense of community among travelers.

Our guides are not just experts in navigating challenging terrains; they are storytellers, naturalists, and passionate advocates for the environments we explore.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Authentic Adventure

We seek out off-the-beaten-path destinations and genuine experiences that challenge and inspire our guests.

Uncompromising Safety

Your well-being is our top priority. We employ rigorous safety protocols and highly trained wilderness first responders.

Sustainability

We practice Leave No Trace principles and actively support the conservation of the environments we visit.

Community

We build lifelong connections between our guests, guides, and the local communities we partner with globally.
