Trip Overview

This day trip down the lower 13-mile stretch of the South Fork American River offers some of the most exciting Class II & III whitewater in the state and is perfect for beginners, families, staff outings, and anyone who wants a day out paddling in the California sunshine. Enjoy a fresh and tasty picnic lunch at our campground before you gear up and hit the river. The trip begins with several miles of bouncy, stress-free rapids before things get a little spicier in the Gorge with big waves, challenging chutes, drops, and unforgettable rapids like Satan’s Cesspool and Hospital Bar.

Whether you’re new to rafting and just want to “get your feet wet,” or you only have time for a quick getaway, our 1-day South Fork American River rafting trip will surely be a perfect choice. About an hour from Sacramento, and just an hour and a half from Lake Tahoe, rafting the South Fork of the American River is a quick and relatively easy option for an exciting family or group adventure. Splashy good times are guaranteed.

Trip Highlight

  • South Fork American River rafting Gorge trips are perfect for families & groups
  • Paddle exciting Class III rapids like Fowler’s Rock, Satan’s Cesspool & Hospital Bar
  • Cool off in the river while swimming through splashy riffles & calm pools
  • Raft the lower South Fork American River through rolling, wooded hills & rocky gorges
  • Affordable camping options with showers & upgraded restrooms available at OARS American River Outpost and OARS at EarthTrek Camp

Itinerary & Map

Itinerary at a Glance

We pride ourselves in running a relaxed and flexible schedule. Every American River rafting trip is different depending upon the group, other trips on the water, and sometimes the weather. The following is a sample of what your trip might be like:

  • 1
    11 AM — Arrive & Gear Up

    Meet at the OARS American River Outpost. Check in with your trip leader, then head to Upper Camp for a tasty picnic lunch. Your guides will deliver a safety talk and fit you with helmet and PFD (wetsuits and splash tops included on cold-weather trips). Walk down to the beach and launch!

  • 2
    Early Afternoon — Warm-Up Rapids

    The first part of the day starts mellow with Class II warm-up rapids like Barking Dog and Highway Rapid. Practice paddling and enjoy swimming opportunities including Swimmers' Rapid before heading into the Gorge.

  • 3
    Mid Afternoon — Enter the Gorge

    Watch for the Lollipop Tree — the official entrance to the Gorge! Canyon walls constrict the river creating an action-packed series of wild Class III rapids: Fowler's Rock, Satan's Cesspool, and Hospital Bar will get your pulse racing. Smile for the photographers!

  • 4
    ~5:30 PM — Take-Out & Return

    After a possible "Surprise" rapid finale, guides may tie boats together for a short tow-out to Salmon Falls Bridge. Shuttle vehicles return everyone to camp. Stay as long as you like — our riverside hideaway is surrounded by Gold Rush history and recreational activities.
